Tile

Clean lines. Even joints.

Tile installation demands precision, technical accuracy, and proper surface preparation. We approach every project with attention to detail, ensuring durable, long-lasting results and a flawless finish.

  1. Surface preparation. Inspection, leveling, and priming.
  2. Layout. Accurate planning for balanced, visually clean results.
  3. Installation. Professional tile work with strict control of alignment and flatness.
  4. Grout & moisture protection. Built for long-term performance.
  5. Final cleaning. Quality inspection before we leave.

Where we work: bathrooms, shower areas, kitchen backsplashes, and floors and walls in moisture-prone spaces. Ceramic, porcelain, and mosaic. Strict compliance with installation standards and approved materials.

Epoxy Floors in Colorado

Strength, style, and a floor that lasts.

Epoxy flooring is one of the most durable and visually appealing solutions for garages, basements, workshops, commercial spaces, and modern residential interiors. We install smooth, seamless, and highly durable epoxy systems built to last for decades.

We use high-quality materials that resist impact, chemicals, moisture, and temperature changes. The coating is easy to clean, dust-free, crack-resistant, and keeps its appearance even under heavy use.

Every epoxy job begins with proper surface preparation: concrete grinding, crack repair, leveling, and priming. Then the system goes down — a base coat, decorative flakes if you want them, and a protective topcoat. The result is a strong, beautiful, fully renewed floor.

Floors & windows

Laminate, wood, and new glass.

Laminate flooring

We install a layer of laminate onto a prepared subfloor, usually chipboard or plywood. Laminate imitates wood and is a popular choice for strength, durability, and relatively low cost. The job includes surface preparation, click-grip laying, cutting, and installing skirting boards.

Wood brushing & OSMO

Wood brushing, aging of wood, and coating with Polyx Oil OSMO Germany — wood, wooden doors, table tops, décor elements. The oil penetrates, leaves pores open so the wood can breathe, resists liquids and ultraviolet light, and is made from natural ingredients.

Window replacement

Window replacement is not just about changing the glass units. It is precise work that affects the warmth, noise levels, and appearance of the home. What matters: proper preparation of the opening, high-quality sealing, precise fitting of the frame, and neat finishing of the slopes.

Turnkey windows

The result is airtight, warm, and visually neat windows that last for many years. We visit the site, take measurements, and carry out turnkey installation.
